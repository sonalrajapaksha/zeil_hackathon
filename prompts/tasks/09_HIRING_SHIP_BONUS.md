**B01 hiring:** Implement demonstrable candidate journey: realistic fictional jobseeker persona with an observable accessibility barrier (use the disabled jobseeker persona required by the bonus description when claiming B01); conversational intake; pending evidence cards; human confirmation; choose fictional job; trustworthy CV and cover letter.
